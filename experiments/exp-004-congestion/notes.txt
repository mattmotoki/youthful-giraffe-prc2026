The rolling-window fix changed how honest the neighbour features are, not just their values:
  nbr_taxiin_rwy populated 64.9 % -> 17.6 %
Arrivals seldom land on a DEPARTURE runway within the preceding 30 minutes -- at a
segregated-mode airport essentially never. The old read-at-the-last-event implementation was
returning the last time any arrival used that runway, often hours earlier, so 47 % of that
column was a stale value dressed as a current one. It is now NaN unless arrivals genuinely used
the departure runway recently, which is exactly the mixed-mode signal the feature was for.
