# Q05 — observable clock and reporting patterns: **NEGATIVE**

**Verdict: negative on the tested heads.** The clock artifacts are real, large and exactly located:
`AOBT_3_flt` is quantised to whole minutes on 98.45 % of rows, while the two label-side clocks
`BLOCK_TIME_UTC_mvt` and `MVT_TIME_UTC_mvt` are quantised on 6.38 % and 5.14 %. The rows off that
grid carry a mean `B − AOBT` that differs by **−434.7 s** from the rows on it. But v10 has already
absorbed the pattern: the same split moves v10's residual by **+4.59 s**, one per cent of the raw
anchor error it would move. Every global phase head loses board — the real 5 s, 15 s and 60 s heads
lose 0.09 to 0.10 s of clean board — and the head that gains, airport × operator, gains from
identity, not from timing.

**What this negative does and does not close.** It closes the **global shrunk phase heads actually
run here** (`sum/(n+200)` group means on blocked day splits, at 5, 15 and 60 s, alone, beyond the
existing timing controls, and beyond airport × operator). It does not close every conceivable
per-group estimator: blocked splits stop a head fitting its own rows, but they do not remove
shrinkage bias or sparse-group bias, and a differently regularised estimator on the same partition
is not tested here. **Revival condition:** stable conditional residual evidence for a phase term
under a capacity-matched estimator on a partition whose group frequencies are matched by
construction.

Everything below is `code/q05.py` on the fold-side v10 of `code/v10.py`, measured through
`code/packet.py` (`analysis/q05.json`, `analysis/packet-q05.json`, log `logs/q05.log`, 6.1 s at
4 threads). Section 9's tables are generated from that record by `code/tables.py`; the report holds
no hand-typed number the record does not carry, and no table outside the generated block.

**Round 1 repairs.** Sol's round 1 found two things. First, the earlier run declared "5 s / 15 s /
60 s phases" but scored only a single 60-second residue head; the real `phase_5s`, `phase_15s` and
`phase_60s` heads now run, and a cardinality-matched sham was added. Second — and this is a claim
that went from wrong to right — the earlier report said **"the anchor absorbs the phase"**. That is
algebraically false: for the AOBT phase split, `Δ(B − AOBT) = −434.7 s`, so the raw
take-off-anchor residual changes by **+434.7 s**, not by +4.6 s. The +4.6 s figure is v10's
residual. **v10 absorbs the phase; the raw anchor does not.** `analysis/q05.json` reports
`delta_y`, `delta_BminusAOBT`, `delta_anchor_error` and `delta_r` side by side (T3) so the two
cannot be confused again.

**Round 2 repairs.** Sol's round 2 found three more, all accepted. (a) The "+0.0014 s phase
increment" was described as a phase-only increment; it is not. The fingerprint block carries the
equality and date-gap flags as well as the three whole-minute indicators, and the two heads are
non-nested partitions under one fixed shrinkage constant, so their difference can be moved by group
size as well as by content. It is now called what it is: a **full-fingerprint contrast**, and the
negative is stated for the tested global heads only. (b) The closure language was too wide and is
replaced by the revival condition above. (c) The report had dropped the queue's "check the feature
inventory first" section and the explicit unmet-artifact dependency; both are restored (§1, §6). The
sham is **not** capacity-matched in the strict sense — its group counts are 1,363 / 1,570 / 1,329
against the fingerprint's 1,479 / 1,532 / 1,371, and occupancy under shrinkage differs — so it is
read as a cardinality-scale control, not as an exact capacity control.

## 1. Feature inventory, first

The queue says to check the inventory before measuring. The shipping stack already carries:

- `prc/tail_features.py:319` `_arrival_day_rate` — **the share of an airport-day's ARRIVALS whose
  on-block equals their schedule**, within 60 s. This is the queue's proposed arrival reporting
  witness, already shipped as `arr_day_ph_rate`, and it is in `NOAC_FEATURES_V2`
  (`prc/tail_model.py:166`), i.e. inside the noac head v3/v4 that serves. Its own docstring records
  it as worth 11 to 13 s of noac leave-one-month-out.
- `arr_within_1h_of_sched`, `arr_within_3h_of_sched`, `n_arr_sched_mvt`, `gap_arr_after_sched`,
  `sched_date_cross` — arrival/schedule coincidence and date-cross flags, also in the noac head.
- No feature anywhere in `prc/` is a 5/15/60-second **phase** of any clock. That gap is real and is
  what §4 tests.

So the queue's "distinct conditional extension" is not the witness itself but the restriction of it
to MISSING-TYPE arrivals, which is an input-only change. That is the arm run in §5.

## 2. The identity, checked next (T1)

On labelled audit rows, `max |y − (T − B)| = 0` on every fold, and
`max |[y − (T − AOBT)] + [B − AOBT]| = 0`. `y` is exactly take-off minus off-block, and the
AOBT-anchored residual is exactly `−(B − AOBT)`. This is the algebra every other number in the
screen is read against, and it is exact in float64, not approximate.

## 3. Quantisation is on the NM clocks, not the label clocks (T2)

The planning clocks are minute-grid quantities by construction: `SCHED_TIME_UTC_mvt` and `IOBT_flt`
are on the minute 100 % of the time, `EOBT_1_flt` and `LOBT_flt` 99.50 % and 99.49 %. `AOBT_3_flt`
is an actual off-block report and is still on the minute grid 98.45 % of the time, which is what
makes the 1.55 % off-grid population interesting: those rows are reported by a different path. The
two label-side clocks are not on the grid at all — `BLOCK_TIME_UTC_mvt` 6.38 %, `MVT_TIME_UTC_mvt`
5.14 %.

## 4. The same flag read on four quantities (T3), and the heads (T4)

For `aobt_mod60` — 98.45 % of rows on the grid — the four quantities move as: mean `y` **+252.0 s**,
mean `B − AOBT` **−434.7 s**, mean RAW take-off-anchor error **+434.7 s**, mean v10 residual
**+4.59 s**. The off-grid rows are a genuinely different population: their taxi-out is 252 s shorter
and their `B − AOBT` offset is 435 s larger. A predictor that anchors on `AOBT_3_flt` and ignores
the phase carries the full 434.7 s of that difference. **v10 carries 4.59 s of it.** The artifact is
real; the current predictor has already learned it, through the operator, airport, runway and
clock-difference features it already holds. The same reading holds at 5 s (+433.6) and 15 s
(+437.2), and for the take-off-side residues `t_mod5` / `t_mod15` / `t_mod60` the raw anchor moves
by only 2.6 / 2.8 / 21.2 s — the take-off clock has no comparable regime.

Two other flags are worth naming. `eq_lobt_iobt` (99.29 % true) moves the raw anchor by −456 s and
v10's residual by −30.1 s: it marks the 0.7 % of rows where the flight plan was revised, and v10
holds most but not all of it. `dategap_aobt_t` (0.08 %, 144 rows) moves `y` by +935 s and v10's
residual by +209 s: rows whose take-off crosses a UTC date boundary from their AOBT are genuinely
mispredicted, but there are 144 of them in a 190,713-row fold.

**Every phase head loses the board** (T4): `phase_only` −0.0593 s primary, `phase_5s` −0.0812,
`phase_15s` −0.0845, `phase_60s` −0.0750, and `phase_beyond_controls` −0.1731. Each head is a
per-group mean of `r` estimated on held-out day blocks and added to v10, scored on all rows, so a
head's loss is not an over-fitting artifact of its own rows; it is that the per-group mean is
noisier than what v10 already carries.

**The decomposition, stated at its true scope.** `airport_operator_only` wins +0.1884 s of clean
board on 234 groups. The full `fingerprint_head` — airport × operator × the whole flag-and-phase
block, 1,479 groups — wins +0.1898 s. The difference is **+0.0014 s**, and it is a
**full-fingerprint contrast**, not a phase-only increment: the fingerprint block contains the
equality and date-gap flags too, the two partitions are non-nested, and one fixed shrinkage constant
serves both, so group size can suppress a difference as well as content can. A sham head at
comparable cardinality (1,363 groups) wins +0.1350 s, less than `airport_operator_only` on 234
groups, so cardinality alone does not explain the fingerprint's gain either. And when the phase
block is added on top of those controls explicitly, it is negative: `phase_beyond_airport_operator`
drops from +0.1884 to +0.0063, and `phase_beyond_controls` from −0.0343 to −0.2081.

## 5. The arrival-reporting witness (T5)

The queue's "recording regimes" consultation asks whether a per-airport reporting regime is visible
in the `noac` rows (`AIRCRAFT_TYPE_mvt` null). It is visible and it is thin: the placeholder rate
among noac rows is 0.32 / 0.75 / 0.40 by fold, on 339 / 89 / 63 rows. Restricting the arrival stream
to arrivals with a missing type — the shipped comparison used all arrivals — changes the correlation
with the placeholder from −0.009 to +0.022 on july_loo and from −0.075 to −0.177 on january: it
flips sign across folds. Incremental R² of the missing-type stream over the all-arrival stream is
0.0005 / 0.048 / 0.027 on 339 / 89 / 63 rows. There is a reporting regime; there is not enough of it
to fit anything on.

## 6. The artifact dependency is unmet, and is stated as the queue requires

The witness needs the shipped `p`, `off_np`, the pre-override predictions and the exact override
masks. A search of every committed parquet in `experiments/` finds exactly one of them:
`band_p_rollover` in `experiments/exp-009-noac2/work/v4_july_pred.parquet`, july only.
`p_placeholder`, `y_pred_tail`, `y_pred_bulk` and `p_placeholder_pop` are produced by
`prc/tail_model.py:609–652` at serving time and are committed nowhere. Without them this screen can
measure the witness against v10's residual, which it did, but **cannot** measure it against the gate
it would change. The witness arm is therefore not closed; it is blocked, and the block is a missing
artifact, not a negative result.

## 7. Support and ambiguity

- **The negative is about the increment, not the artifact.** Nothing here says the phase is
  uninformative about `y` — it moves `y` by 252 s. It says v10 already holds all but 4.6 s of it,
  and that adding it back as one of these global shrunk heads costs board.
- **Support is ample for §3 and §4** (118,775 to 143,906 scored rows a fold) and **absent for §5** (63 to
  339 rows).
- **The estimator is a confound the screen does not fully remove.** All heads share one shrinkage
  constant and one blocked-split scheme. That is enough to price *these* heads honestly and not
  enough to price the partition itself; the revival condition at the top says so.
- **`airport_operator_only` is a positive result inside a negative screen**, and it is not this
  screen's to advance: +0.1884 s of clean board from a held-out per-group residual mean on
  airport × operator is a Q03-shaped finding (identity structure v10 under-fits), not a
  clock-artifact finding. Its january arm is negative (−24.0 s²), so it is a candidate feature, not
  a result.
- **The 5 s and 15 s heads have fewer groups than 60 s** (28 and 177 against 366) because the
  residues collapse on the minute grid. Their losses are consistent with one another, so the result
  is not a grid-resolution accident.
- **Selection.** The flag list, the residue moduli, the group definitions and the block splits are
  predeclared in `analysis/q05.json` under `predeclared`. The sham's cardinality is sized from the
  fingerprint's actual populated group count, not chosen; it is not an exact capacity match.

## 8. Current-predictor benefit and the next repair

**Benefit: none from the phase.** The headline arm (`phase_only`) scores 309.1365 against v10's
309.0772 on the primary board, a loss of 0.0593 s.

**Next repair or fit.**

1. **Set the global phase heads `closed-provisional`**, with the cause named: the artifact is real
   and v10 absorbs 99 % of it, so the residual increment is smaller than the estimation noise of the
   per-group heads that could carry it. Revival as stated at the top of this report.
2. **Carry `airport_operator_only` to Q03's follow-up fit** as a candidate feature, priced there
   under the same zero-sum contract, not as a standalone head.
3. **Attach the missing witness artifacts to Q09.** Committing the noac head's shipped `p`,
   `off_np`, pre-override predictions and override masks with a durable address is a one-run task on
   the next noac serving run, and it is what unblocks §5.
4. **Do not fit on the noac reporting regime.** 63 january rows with a sign-flipping correlation is
   not a lane; it is a note in the record.
