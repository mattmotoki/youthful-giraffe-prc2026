# Q04 — do arrivals reveal current departure errors: **NEGATIVE**, with one MAYBE

**Verdict: negative.** The arrival innovation is a real sensor — it agrees with itself across a
split half of each cell's arrival stream at r = 0.25 to 0.31 — but it does not point at v10's
current departure error. Its correlation with the residual is +0.013 / −0.008 / −0.009. Against the
**complete shipped current feature block** as the base (89 columns from the member pipeline, plus
v10 and this screen's own cell summaries — 108 features), adding the innovation moves the increment
by −45.65 / +5.94 / +0.05 s² per scored row: negative on july_loo and inside noise on the other two.
Every arm loses the primary board, `h1` by 0.0592 s. And on the one support where the 7-day-shifted
placebo carries a real value on every row it is scored on, `h1` and `h1c` are not separable: their
increments over the same base are +4.67 / −1.27 / +194.84 and −20.62 / +5.42 / +166.94 s², inside
intervals hundreds of s² wide.

**One MAYBE.** Restricted to cells with at least 10 arrivals, `mean(r·z)` is +2,044 s²
[+770, +3,566] on july_loo, +1,207 s² [−239, +2,821] on december and +32 s² [−1,442, +1,388] on
january. The sign is the same on all three, one interval clears zero, and the pattern is monotone in
arrival count on two folds. **Revival condition:** a cross-month-stable positive sign for
`mean(r·z)` at n_arr ≥ 10 on a month outside {2025-07, 2025-12, 2025-01}. That test is **not free**
and its dependency is stated rather than hidden: a new month has no fold-side v10 residual, so it
needs a v10 replay on that month, and the nuisance model must be refitted excluding it. Only
3,083 / 1,721 / 939 rows qualify here, which is 1.6 % / 1.0 % / 0.6 % of the fold.

Everything below is `code/q04.py` on the fold-side v10 of `code/v10.py`, measured through
`code/packet.py` (`analysis/q04.json`, `analysis/packet-q04.json`, log `logs/q04.log`, 848 s at
4 threads). Section 8's tables are generated from that record by `code/tables.py`; the report holds
no hand-typed number the record does not carry.

**Round 1 repairs.** Sol's round 1 found four things: `h0` too thin, departure counts computed over
all regimes, the coverage census including EHAM/EDDF, and `h1c` contaminated by keeping the real
`zprev` alongside the shifted block. The last three were repaired then.

**Round 2 repairs.** Sol's round 2 found the first one only half repaired and two more, all
accepted.

1. **`h0` was still not the base the queue names.** The first repair grew it to 19 hand-built
   columns, which is matched *learner* capacity but not "all current arrival, runway, clock and
   window summaries". `h0` now carries the **shipped block itself** — the 89 numeric feature columns
   built by `experiments/exp-018-fresh/code/features.py` and `window_features.py`, whose own
   contract is this screen's contract (departure `BLOCK_TIME_UTC_mvt` and `TAXITIME_SEC_mvt` are
   never read; arrival rows keep every column). The cache file's SHA-256 is in the record per fold.
   `h0` goes from 19 to 108 features and its july_loo increment moves from −171.2 to −156.8 s².
2. **`h1c` was not a sound matched-support placebo.** Only 38.5 % / 39.4 % / 35.3 % of the currently
   supported rows also meet the shifted support rule, so a zero-filled shifted column encoded
   *availability*, not a placebo value. Both shifted columns are now gated on the shifted cell's own
   declared support, and every arm is scored a second time on the **intersection** of the two
   supports (T4b). The shift is **+7 days, later**; the round-1 report said "earlier" and was wrong
   about the direction.
3. **The MAYBE was not operational as written.** "One pass over one month, no refitting" was false.
   The revival condition above now names its two dependencies.

## 1. What was run

The eligible set is `nm_bulk` outside EHAM and EDDF. A departure row is **supported** when its
airport × 10-minute cell holds at least 6 eligible departures and at least 6 arrivals; that is
25.77 % / 23.93 % / 19.50 % of the eligible rows (T1), across 4,664 / 4,037 / 3,138 cells and all 31
dates of each fold.

The arrival innovation is `z = taxi_in − g(...)`, where `g` is a hierarchical nuisance model fitted
**on the other eleven months only** — 1.89 M to 1.93 M arrivals per fold — with back-off over
`(apt, rwy, pier, wtc) → (apt, rwy, pier) → (apt, rwy, hour) → (apt, rwy) → (apt, hour) → (apt)`.
The scoring month never contributes to its own nuisance. `z` is then aggregated to the cell and to a
fixed-width wide window over an explicit consecutive-bin grid.

Four arms, all gradient-boosted at the same capacity (64 depth-2 trees, lr 0.05, seed 39), all
fitted to predict `r` from features only:

- **h0** — the shipped current block (89 columns) plus v10's prediction, the two clock differences
  and this screen's own cell summaries: 108 features. The base the queue names.
- **h1** — h0 plus the two innovation features (cell and wide window). 110 features.
- **h1c** — h0 plus the same two features computed from the innovation **7 days later**, both gated
  on the shifted cell's own declared support. The placebo: same capacity, same shape, no current
  information.
- **h1_shrunk** — h1 with the innovation shrunk by cell arrival count.

Each arm is scored twice: on the **current** support (the primary comparison, T4) and on the
**intersection** of the current and shifted supports (where the placebo is well defined, T4b).

## 2. The sensor is real (T2)

Split each cell's arrivals into two halves by arrival order and centre each half by the OFF-MONTH
nuisance model — never by its own stream mean, which would manufacture agreement. The two halves'
innovations correlate at **0.2776 / 0.2476 / 0.3068** across 9,512 / 7,498 / 6,249 cells. There is a
common per-cell arrival-side quantity and the model measures it.

## 3. It does not point at the departure error (T3)

On the supported rows, `corr(r, z)` is **+0.013 / −0.0083 / −0.0093** and `mean(r·z)` is
+305.6 [−182.4, +797.7] / −123.8 [−540.2, +264.0] / −347.2 [−1086.6, +379.8] s². Two folds are
negative. No interval excludes zero.

**The contrast that shows the ceiling is not the sensor.** Replace `z` with the self-excluded cell
mean of the OTHER departures' residuals — an oracle, because it reads departure labels that the
ranking mask removes, so it can never be served — and the same cross-product is
**+5,850 / +4,214 / +4,797 s²**, every interval clear of zero by a wide margin. The shared
within-cell departure error that Q03 exploits is there and is large. Arrivals do not see it.

## 4. Against the complete base, every arm loses (T4, T5, T7)

The primary endpoint is the paired ALL-ROW january/july board. `h1` scores 309.1365 against v10's
309.0772, a loss of **0.0592 s**; `h0` loses 0.0519 s; `h1c` 0.0548 s; `h1_shrunk` 0.0584 s. On the
secondary clean board the same ordering holds (−0.0712 / −0.0624 / −0.0659 / −0.0702 s).

**The increment over h0 is the measurement that matters.** `h1 − h0` is
**−45.65 / +5.94 / +0.05 s²** per scored row. The largest of the three is a loss on the heavier
board month; the other two are inside a bootstrap interval that spans hundreds of s². Nothing here
is a stable positive increment, which is what the queue's "Advance on" clause requires.

The completed base is what makes this reading fair, and it moved the numbers: with the 19-column
base of round 1 the same increments were −30.94 / +27.96 / −10.39 s². The stronger base absorbs part
of what the innovation looked like it was adding on december and takes the july loss further
negative.

## 5. The placebo, read where it is well defined (T4b)

On the intersection support — 13,096 / 11,603 / 7,949 rows, 35 % to 39 % of the currently supported
rows — every arm is close to v10 (h0 −0.0007 s, h1 +0.0068 s, h1c +0.0042 s, h1_shrunk +0.0028 s on
the primary board), and the increments over `h0` are:

| arm | july_loo | december | january |
|---|---|---|---|
| h1 | +4.67 | −1.27 | +194.84 |
| h1c | −20.62 | +5.42 | +166.94 |

Both rows sit inside per-arm intervals of ±200 to ±800 s². **`h1` and `h1c` are not separable on
this support.** A 7-day-shifted copy of the innovation, which cannot contain the current answer,
buys within noise of what the current innovation buys. That is the specificity check the queue asks
for, and it fails to distinguish the sensor from its own weekly shadow.

The round-1 report claimed the placebo *beat* the real innovation. On the repaired construction and
the completed base that is no longer the finding; the finding is that the two cannot be told apart.
The round-1 statement rested on a placebo whose zeroes encoded availability, and it is withdrawn.

## 6. Support and ambiguity

- **Coverage is the honest limit on the negative.** Only about a quarter of the eligible rows have
  6 arrivals and 6 departures in their cell, and the median cell holds 4 to 5 arrivals (T1). A
  sensor built from 5 arrivals is thin. The screen tests what the data supports, not what a busier
  cell would support.
- **The matched support is denser and behaves differently.** On it, july_loo's arms are all
  positive (+195 to +220 s²) and december's all negative (−226 to −239 s²), for `h0` as much as for
  `h1`; that is the base moving with the cohort, not the innovation. It is the reason the placebo
  comparison is read as "not separable" rather than as either direction of result.
- **The MAYBE is the coverage limit read forward.** The reliability repair (T6) walks the arrival
  threshold up: at n_arr ≥ 8 the cross-product is +517 / +281 / −658 s²; at ≥ 10 it is
  +2,044 / +1,207 / +32; at ≥ 12 (july_loo only, 247 rows) +4,788 [−6, +10,767]. The sign turns
  positive on all three folds at ≥ 10 and the magnitude grows with density on two. That is either a
  real sensor that needs arrivals to be legible, or the tail of a thin slice. 939 january rows
  cannot separate those.
- **The thresholds were lowered after the first run.** 12/20/40 with a 500-row minimum returned
  empty because the median cell holds 4 to 5 arrivals; they are now 8/10/12/15 with a 200-row
  minimum. That is a selection on the fold data and it is why the MAYBE carries a revival condition
  on an unused month rather than a gate result.
- **The nuisance model is clean.** It excludes the scoring month entirely, so no arrival in the
  scored month contributes to its own centring. Its size and back-off levels are in
  `analysis/q04.json` under `folds.<fold>.nuisance_train_arrivals` and `predeclared`.
- **The oracle is named as one.** The self-excluded departure cross-product reads departure labels
  and is reported only to bound what a within-cell signal could be worth. Nothing in any served arm
  uses it.
- **The base is an input, and its address is recorded.** The shipped block comes from
  `artifacts/exp-018-fresh/cache/<month>.parquet`, which is a build cache and not committed; its
  SHA-256 per fold is in the record (`folds.<fold>.W_cache_sha256`) and the committed code that
  builds it is `experiments/exp-018-fresh/code/features.py`. No row failed to join
  (`W_missing_rows` = 0 on all three folds).

## 7. Current-predictor benefit

**None.** Every arm loses the primary board on the current support; on the matched support every
arm, base included, is within 0.007 s of v10. There is no benefit to take.

## 8. Next repair or fit

1. **Do not fit a member on the arrival innovation.** Against the complete current base the
   increment is negative on the heavier board month and inside noise elsewhere, and it cannot be
   told apart from its own 7-day shadow.
2. **The one repair worth its cost** is the MAYBE's revival test, with its dependencies paid:
   a v10 replay on one month outside the three folds, a nuisance refit excluding that month, then
   `mean(r·z)` at n_arr ≥ 10 with the same threshold and no other change. Keep it in the backlog
   with that condition, not as an open lane.
3. **The queue's routing holds.** Q04's failure branch sends the within-cell question back to Q03,
   which is where the +5,850 s² self-excluded oracle says the shared error actually lives, and Q03
   reaches part of it without reading any label.
