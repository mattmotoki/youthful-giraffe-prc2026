# Q03 — relative errors inside an airport-window cell: **ADVANCE**

**Verdict: advance.** Flight-level error structure survives after the shared airport-window error is
removed, and it is worth **+0.0782 s on the primary all-row january/july board** over v10 at an
amplitude fitted on other months (+0.0940 s on the secondary clean board). The label-free pair
diagnostic — the one that estimates no held-out cell mean — is positive on all three folds with
every interval clear of zero, and stays clear under 1-, 2- and 3-day moving blocks drawn inside each
month. Two controls at matched support and matched budget do not reproduce it: the uncentered ridge
is negative pooled, and the same fitted direction served without the projection costs 3.1 s of
board.

Everything below is `code/q03.py` on the fold-side v10 of `code/v10.py`, measured through
`code/packet.py` (`analysis/q03.json` and `analysis/packet-q03.json`, log `logs/q03.log`, 63.4 s at
4 threads, no refit of any member). Every table in section 8 is generated from that record by
`code/tables.py`; the report holds no hand-typed number that the record does not carry.

**Round 1 repairs.** Sol's round 1 found the pair evidence read at the out-of-block amplitude
(an indirect path from the scored block into the ridge) and the uncentered control read on a
different row support. Both are repaired: the headline pair row is now the **cross-month**
amplitude, and the uncentered control is restricted to the same 110,691 / 101,449 / 91,861 rows the
zero-sum arm updates. A `projection_off` ablation was added. The direction and the sign of the
screen are unchanged; the pair point estimate rose from +179.47 to +199.07 s² pooled.

**Round 2 repairs.** Sol's round 2 found three more, all accepted. (a) `packet.primary()` rounded
each fold RMSE to four decimals *before* the weighted board aggregation, so the headline inherited
up to 1e-4 s of rounding per fold; the board is now aggregated on the raw float64 RMSE and the
headline is **+0.0782 s**, not +0.0781. (b) The pooled moving-block bootstrap concatenated the three
months into one 93-label sequence, so a 2- or 3-day block could straddle a July/December/January
boundary that does not exist in time; blocks are now drawn inside each fold and the sampled
numerators and counts are pooled (pooled 3-day interval [+133.1, +278.4], previously
[+117.2, +274.0]). (c) The claim "only the zero-sum constraint differs" was too strong and is
withdrawn — see §5.

## 1. What was run

Eligibility is the `nm_bulk` ordinary joined regime outside EHAM and EDDF, exactly as the queue sets
it. `nm_late`, `nonm`, `noac` and those two airports receive **zero** update. The cell is airport ×
UTC 10-minute bin × serving regime; the regime is constant inside the eligible set, so the cell is
airport × bin. The eligible movement ids are frozen before any feature work and addressed by the
SHA-256 of their sorted list (T1).

A fixed ridge (202 row-only linear features, α = 100 on standardised columns) is fitted on four of
five contiguous day blocks with **feature and target centring inside the fitting split only**. At
inference the update is `d_i = h(X_i) − mean_cell(h(X))` over the complete eligible scoring cohort,
with uniform weights and features only: no departure residual and no label is read at scoring time.
Cells with fewer than four eligible rows get zero. `d` is added after the serving mask and the
ORIGINAL all-row residuals are scored.

The measurement contract is the shared one: `r = y − p_v10`, `d = p_cand − p_v10`, `A = mean(r·d)`,
`B = mean(d²)`, full-amplitude gain `2A − B`, unrestricted scale `A/B`. All departure `BLOCK_TIME`
and `y` are masked by `prc.data.mask_like_ranking` before any context is built.

## 2. The two algebraic checks (T2)

Both hold at the 1e−8 relative tolerance the queue names, on all three folds. The second is the
"mean-error contribution stays unchanged" clause: because every scored cell's update sums to zero,
the change in squared error is identical whether it is computed on the raw residual or on the
within-cell-centred residual. Both are computed on the full eligible set, not on a clean-only
subset.

## 3. The pair diagnostic — no held-out cell mean is estimated (T3)

Fixed disjoint within-cell pairs on held-out days, formed by sorting on cell then movement id, so
the pairing uses no feature and no label. The statistic is the squared error of the residual
DIFFERENCE, before and after subtracting `h_i − h_j`. 51,280 / 47,070 / 42,561 pairs. Intervals are
day-clustered bootstraps, 2,000 draws, seed 39, with 1/2/3-day moving-block intervals alongside.

The full-amplitude row is positive on all three folds but resolves on none: the unrestricted scale
`A/B` is 0.555 / 0.619 / 0.533, so the ridge's raw output is roughly twice the size the residual
supports and the `−B` term eats the gain. With one scalar amplitude fitted **on the other two
months** — never on the rows it scores, and never on the fold that scores them — the same contrast
is positive and clear of zero on every fold and the rescaled `A/B` moves to 1.01 / 1.12 / 0.91. That
is the check that the amplitude, not the direction, was what was wrong.

**Why the cross-month row and not the out-of-block one.** The out-of-block amplitude for block *b*
is fitted on the other blocks' rows, so no scored row contributes to its own weight — but those
rows' `h` values came from ridge fits that each excluded only their OWN block, and therefore saw
block *b*. The dependence is indirect and weak, and it is still a dependence. The cross-month
amplitude has none of it: each fold's ridge is fitted inside its own month, so the amplitude applied
to july_loo is a function of december and january rows only. Both rows are reported; the screen
advances on the cross-month one.

## 4. The full zero-sum update, scored on all rows (T4, T5, T7)

**The amplitude transfers across months.** Fitted on the other two folds only, it is 0.5478 for
july_loo, 0.5523 for december and 0.5831 for january — a 6 % spread across a summer month, a winter
month and a January.

304,001 rows across the three folds carry a nonzero update; the rest are in cells below the four-row
minimum and are left alone. The primary endpoint is the paired ALL-ROW january/july board at
exp-016's ranking weights (0.5571 / 0.4429); the clean board and december are secondary. Per-date
and per-row contributions are in `analysis/dates-q03-cross-<fold>.csv`.

## 5. The two negative controls

- **Uncentered: a matched-support, matched-budget control, not a one-term ablation.** Same features,
  same α, same splits, same day blocks, same 110,691 / 101,449 / 91,861 rows. It is **not** the
  centred arm minus one constraint: it is a separately fitted ridge, so its training objective,
  its target centring and its intercept all differ as well. Read it as "the same feature budget
  spent without the within-cell contrast", which is what it is. Pooled it is
  **-7.93 s² [-114.0, +94.3]**, its january arm is **−199.89 s² [−365.4, −31.5]** with an interval
  that excludes zero, and its pair statistic is negative on two folds and pooled. So what carries
  here is specifically the WITHIN-cell contrast, not a generic row-level correction of v10.
- **Projection off.** The identical fitted `h`, at the identical cross-month amplitude, on the
  identical rows, applied *without* subtracting the cell mean, is **−2,607 s² pooled** and
  **−3.1056 s of all-row board**. This is a one-term ablation and it is the stronger of the two
  controls, but its scope is exactly what it tests: for **this** fitted direction the projection is
  necessary. It does not establish that zero-sum projection is the mechanism for every candidate;
  it establishes that this candidate's gain is entirely in the within-cell contrast and that its
  unprojected part is actively harmful.

## 6. Support and ambiguity

- **Coefficient stability is moderate, not high.** The cosine between the five fitting splits'
  coefficient vectors has minimum 0.41 / 0.68 / 0.58 and mean 0.78 / 0.79 / 0.74. The direction is
  reproducible enough to transfer across months at a stable amplitude, but the individual
  coefficients are not stable, and the top-weighted features differ by fold (runway contrasts and
  `d_aobt_lobt` / `d_t_eobt` in july_loo and january, operator and market-segment terms in
  december).
- **The cell-size strata do not line up (T6).** At the cross-month amplitude, cells of 4–5 rows give
  +233 / +414 / +381 s² and cells of 10–19 rows give −81 / +18 / +146 s². The gain is concentrated
  in small cells and the large-cell stratum changes sign. This is the one place where the
  density-confound repair the queue names ("test fixed-size matched subsets and a fixed alternate
  grid") has something to answer.
- **Multi-day dependence does not remove it.** Pooled pair intervals at 1-, 2- and 3-day moving
  blocks, with every block drawn **inside one month**, are [+131.0, +263.3], [+137.3, +268.7] and
  [+133.1, +278.4]; the cell statistic behaves the same way. Widening the block moves the bounds by
  a few s² and never reaches zero.
- **Selection.** Nothing in this screen is chosen on the scored rows: the cell grid, the four-row
  minimum, α, the block count and the pairing rule were all predeclared and are in
  `analysis/q03.json` under `predeclared`. The amplitude is the only fitted scalar and it is fitted
  off the scored rows and off the scored month.
- **What this is not.** +0.0782 s of fold board is a fold number on a candidate that has not been
  put through a serving path. exp-036's transfer of 0.26× face (BOARD READING 9) is the most recent
  evidence on what an ordinary-row fold gain is worth on the board, so this is not a 0.078 s board
  claim.

## 7. Current-predictor benefit and the next fit

**Benefit.** +0.0782 s on the primary all-row january/july board (309.0772 → 308.9991), +0.0940 s on
the secondary clean board (257.0248 → 256.9308); all three folds positive, all three paired
intervals clear of zero, on 304,001 updated rows. Against the 2.115 s gap to the leader this is
3.7 % of the distance at face value, and it is the only one of the four screens with a positive
current-predictor benefit.

**Next repair or fit.**

1. **The fit this earns** (the queue's "Positive complete-cohort and pair evidence earns a new
   learner"): replace the fixed ridge with a capacity-matched learner on the same zero-sum
   objective, and put the update into a serving path so it can be priced against v10 as a real
   candidate under a Sol round. Keep the amplitude fitted cross-month.
2. **The density check the strata ask for**, before that fit: fixed-size matched subsets and one
   fixed alternate grid (5-minute and 20-minute bins), to answer whether the small-cell
   concentration is cohort density rather than the mechanism.
3. **Do not route to Q04.** The queue's failure branch ("if only cell means help, route to Q04") is
   not the case here: the cell mean is projected out by construction and the gain survives.
4. **State the control's scope when this is quoted.** The evidence is "this fitted direction needs
   the projection" plus "the same budget spent uncentred does not pay"; it is not a general claim
   about zero-sum projections.
