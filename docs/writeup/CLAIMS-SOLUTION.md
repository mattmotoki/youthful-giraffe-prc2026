# Sources for the solution story

This map covers [SOLUTION.md](SOLUTION.md) (version 5, final entry upload 73) and its ten figures.
The snapshot is the 06:52 UTC board of 8 October 2026. Public scores are RMSE in seconds; local gains are
reductions in mean squared error per row (G, s² per row) on held January and July 2025 with 2026
cell weights. Gains measured against different parents are not added. Each rounded value in the
story is mapped below to its exact source value.

Sources, in the order a reader can check them:

- the [record extract](figures/src/evidence.json): our own upload scores (uploads 1 to 75),
  coverage counts, clock timing, early-stop rounds, the alignment ladder, stack weights and the
  board snapshot, each with its source path inside the repository; no other team's details;
- the [figure script](figures/src/build_figures.py) and its output
  [descriptive-statistics.json](figures/src/descriptive-statistics.json): statistics recomputed
  from the twelve official 2025 training files (SHA-256 values stored) and from the extract; the
  script fits no model;
- the Mermaid source of Figure 10, [10-pipeline.mmd](figures/src/10-pipeline.mmd);
- the technical reference [METHOD.md](METHOD.md), cited by section; line numbers refer to the
  METHOD.md of this version (final entry upload 73);
- the public submission extract [PUBLIC-SUBMISSIONS.md](../PUBLIC-SUBMISSIONS.md), rows 1 to 75
  with file hashes and board scores (row N = upload N);
- inside the repository only: the submission log `docs/SUBMISSIONS.md` (rows 1 to 72 have the
  upload number; uploads 73, 74 and 75 are rows 78, 79 and 81; each row has the candidate file,
  local reads and forecasts), the lever log `docs/LEVERS.md`, the board snapshots under
  `docs/board/`, the two worker reports behind uploads 68 to 75
  (`docs/status/2026-10-08-release-v73/isolated-1-REPORT.md` and `isolated-2-REPORT.md`), and
  experiment and test reports.

## Rebuild and visual checks

From the repository root, with the official training files in `data/raw/`:

```bash
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=2 OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 \
  .venv/bin/python docs/writeup/figures/src/build_figures.py
```

The script writes figures 1 to 9 and `descriptive-statistics.json`; a second run gives the same
files. Figure 10 is rendered from its Mermaid source with the command in that file's header, on
the Mac only, because the write-up is not public yet. Every PNG was opened at 800 pixels wide.
The [check record](figures/src/checks.json) gives, per figure, what a reader should see in five
seconds and whether it does, with the size and SHA-256. No figure shows a flight identifier, call
sign, registration or exact flight date.
